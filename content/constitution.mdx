Constitution of the DIA Programming Club (DPC)

Status: DRAFT v0.1, subject to approval by the Daffodil International Academy authority
Last amended: [date]

Preamble

We, the students of Daffodil International Academy, establish the DIA Programming Club to build a community where students learn to code, build real things, compete, and grow together. We commit to curiosity, collaboration, integrity, and open learning for every student regardless of program, year, or skill level.

Article 1: Name and Nature

1.1 The name of the club is the DIA Programming Club, abbreviated DPC.
1.2 DPC is a student-led, non-profit, non-political club of Daffodil International Academy (DIA), Dhaka, operating under the approval and guidance of the DIA authority.
1.3 DPC’s tagline is = new instance of future();.
1.4 DPC’s official website is diapc.dev.

Article 2: Aims and Objectives

2.1 To promote programming, software development, and emerging technology among DIA students.
2.2 To organize workshops, bootcamps, contests, hackathons, seminars, and study circles.
2.3 To prepare members for industry through projects, mentorship, and professional certifications.
2.4 To represent DIA in inter-university and national competitions.
2.5 To build partnerships with other clubs, communities, universities, and industry.
2.6 To create an inclusive environment where beginners are welcomed and supported.

Article 3: Membership

3.1 General Member: any currently enrolled DIA student who registers and agrees to this constitution and the Code of Conduct.
3.2 Associate Member: a non-DIA participant (e.g. from a partner club) who joins specific activities. Associates cannot vote or hold office.
3.3 Alumni Member: a former DIA student who stays connected to the club. Alumni may mentor and advise but cannot vote or hold office.
3.4 Honorary Member: a person invited by the Executive Council for exceptional contribution to the club or the field.
3.5 Membership is free unless a specific activity states otherwise in advance.
3.6 Rights of General Members: attend club activities, join wings, propose events and projects, vote in elections and general meetings, stand for office once eligible, and receive participation certificates where offered.
3.7 Duties: follow the Code of Conduct, respect DIA rules, and take part in activities in good faith.
3.8 Membership ends on resignation, graduation (becoming Alumni), or removal under Article 10.

Article 4: Governance Structure

4.1 Faculty Advisor. The DIA authority appoints one or more Faculty Advisors to guide the club. The Advisor reviews major decisions, approves finances above the threshold in Article 8, and acts as the link between the club and the DIA authority.

4.2 Executive Council. The council consists of:

President
Vice President
General Secretary
Joint Secretary
Treasurer
Head of Wings (coordinates all wings)

4.3 Department Leads. The Executive Council may appoint leads for departments such as Events and Operations, Technical and Projects, Design and Media, Public Relations and Partnerships, Content and Communications, and Web and Digital Platforms. Departments may be added or merged as needed.

4.4 Roles in brief

President: leads the club, chairs meetings, represents DPC officially.
Vice President: supports the President and acts in their absence.
General Secretary: keeps records, minutes, and the member register, and manages official correspondence.
Joint Secretary: assists the General Secretary and coordinates departments.
Treasurer: manages funds, budgets, and financial records.
Head of Wings: oversees wing leads and wing activities, and reviews wing proposals.

4.5 No person may hold more than one Executive Council position at the same time.

Article 5: Wings

5.1 A Wing is a focused sub-community within DPC dedicated to one domain (for example cloud computing, competitive programming, web and app development, AI and machine learning, cybersecurity, game development, or design).
5.2 Founding wing: the AWS Student Builder Group at DIA is DPC’s first wing. It operates under both this constitution and the rules of the AWS Student Builder Groups program. Where AWS program rules apply to the group’s use of AWS names, logos, or resources, those rules prevail for that matter.
5.3 Creating a wing. Any group of at least [5] members may propose a wing with a written charter stating its purpose, planned activities, and proposed Wing Lead. The Executive Council reviews the proposal and, with the Faculty Advisor’s consent, approves it.
5.4 Wing Lead. Each wing has a Wing Lead, who runs the wing and reports to the Head of Wings. Wing Leads serve a term of [one year] and may organize their own core team.
5.5 Wing activity. A wing that holds no activity for [two consecutive semesters] may be placed in dormant status by the Executive Council, with the Wing Lead given the chance to respond first.
5.6 Wings may have their own branding within the DPC brand system, and appear on the DPC website under the Wings section.

Article 6: Elections and Tenure

6.1 Term: Executive Council members serve [one academic year].
6.2 Eligibility: a candidate must be a current General Member in good standing for at least [one semester] (waived for the first election) and not under disciplinary action.
6.3 Election Commission: before each election, the Executive Council, with the Faculty Advisor, forms a neutral Election Commission of [three] people, none of whom are candidates.
6.4 Procedure: nominations are open for at least [7] days, followed by a campaign period and a secret ballot (physical or digital) among General Members. The candidate with the most votes wins. Ties are resolved by a re-vote.
6.5 Vacancies: if an office becomes vacant, the Executive Council appoints an interim holder until the next election or a by-election.
6.6 Removal: an Executive Council member may be removed for serious misconduct or sustained inactivity by a two-thirds vote of the Executive Council and the Faculty Advisor’s approval, or by a two-thirds vote at a General Meeting.
6.7 Founding provisions: until the first election, the club is run by an interim committee led by the Founding Convener, [Ratul Hasan Ruhan], who must call the first election within [six months] of the club’s official approval.

Article 7: Meetings and Decisions

7.1 Executive Council meetings are held at least [monthly]. Quorum is a majority of the Council.
7.2 General Meetings are held at least [once per semester], open to all members. Quorum is [20%] of General Members or [25] members, whichever is lower.
7.3 Decisions are taken by consensus where possible, otherwise by simple majority. The chair holds a casting vote only in a tie.
7.4 The General Secretary circulates the agenda at least [3] days before a meeting and records minutes, which are shared with the Faculty Advisor and kept in the club archive.

Article 8: Finance

8.1 DPC is non-profit. All funds are used only for club activities.
8.2 Sources of funds may include DIA allocations, approved sponsorships, event fees, and donations, all with the Faculty Advisor’s knowledge.
8.3 The Treasurer keeps a record of every income and expense with receipts.
8.4 Any single expense above [BDT amount] needs prior written approval from the Faculty Advisor.
8.5 A financial summary is presented at the end of each term and made available to members on request.
8.6 No office-bearer may receive personal financial benefit from club funds.

Article 9: Collaborations and Partnerships

9.1 DPC may collaborate with other student clubs, universities, communities, companies, and organizations to co-host events and projects, share resources, and create opportunities for members.
9.2 Collaboration requests are submitted through the club’s official channels (including the Collaborate page on diapc.dev) and are reviewed by the Public Relations and Partnerships lead.
9.3 Collaborations that involve funds, use of DIA’s name or premises, or any formal commitment need the Executive Council’s approval and the Faculty Advisor’s consent. A written agreement or MoU is recommended for significant partnerships.
9.4 DPC will not accept any partnership that conflicts with DIA policy, the Code of Conduct, or the club’s non-political and non-profit nature.
9.5 Partners must credit DPC accurately. DPC will credit its partners on event pages and the website.
9.6 Sponsorship never gives a sponsor control over club decisions.

Article 10: Code of Conduct and Discipline

10.1 Members must treat everyone with respect, and must not harass, bully, discriminate, or intimidate anyone, online or offline.
10.2 Cheating, plagiarism, and misrepresentation in competitions and club activities are prohibited. Members must not claim credit for others’ work.
10.3 Members must follow DIA rules, applicable laws, and the terms of any platform or program the club uses.
10.4 Process: a complaint is submitted to the General Secretary or Faculty Advisor, reviewed by a [three]-person panel (including the Faculty Advisor or their nominee), the accused is given the chance to respond, and a decision is recorded.
10.5 Actions range from a warning to temporary suspension, removal from office, or removal from membership, depending on severity. Serious matters are referred to the DIA authority.
10.6 Complaints are handled with confidentiality. Retaliation against a complainant is itself a violation.

Article 11: Digital Assets and Intellectual Property

11.1 The website, domain names, social media accounts, email addresses, repositories, and cloud accounts used by DPC are club assets, held on behalf of the club, not owned by any individual.
11.2 Administrator access to these assets is shared with the Faculty Advisor or a person designated by the DIA authority, and credentials are stored securely by the General Secretary.
11.3 Outgoing office-bearers must hand over all credentials and documentation to their successors within [14] days of the end of their term.
11.4 Projects built by members under club programs remain the intellectual property of their creators, unless a written agreement says otherwise. By participating, members allow the club to display the project and its credits for promotion.
11.5 The club’s name, logo, and brand may be used only with the Executive Council’s permission.
11.6 DPC will not publish a person’s private information or photos without consent, and will respect requests to remove them.

Article 12: Amendments

12.1 An amendment may be proposed in writing by the Executive Council or by [10%] of General Members.
12.2 It must be circulated to members at least [7] days before the vote.
12.3 It passes with a two-thirds majority of members present at a General Meeting with quorum, and takes effect after the Faculty Advisor and the DIA authority approve it.
12.4 The date and content of each amendment is recorded in the amendment log on the website.

Article 13: Dissolution

13.1 DPC may be dissolved by a three-quarters vote at a General Meeting called for that purpose, with the DIA authority’s approval.
13.2 After debts are settled, remaining assets go to DIA or to a similar educational non-profit, as the DIA authority decides.
